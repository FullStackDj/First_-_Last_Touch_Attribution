Stop Letting Direct Visits Steal Credit from Real Acquisition

Example: a prospect arrives from Google Ads, leaves, comes back directly three days later, and submits your Contact Us form. The final Direct visit should not erase the campaign that acquired the prospect. This module keeps First Touch and Latest Qualified Acquisition separate, so the journey stays understandable.

  1. Keep the First Touch: The first observed Website entry after optional-cookie consent is preserved and does not get replaced by later visits.
  2. Track the Latest Qualified Acquisition: A later UTM-tagged visit, supported ad click ID, or external referral can update the latest acquisition without erasing the first.
  3. Keep One Atomic Snapshot: Source, medium, campaign, term, content, landing page, external referrer, click ID, and timestamp stay together as one touch instead of being mixed across visits.
  4. Do Not Reward Navigation: Internal navigation and later Direct visits do not replace a previously known qualified acquisition.
Result: when a customer returns directly before converting, the known acquisition source keeps its place instead of disappearing behind “Direct.”

See Acquisition Where Revenue Is Actually Worked

Attribution is much less useful if it disappears when the CRM lead becomes a customer or quotation. Keep the acquisition context on the records Sales actually opens when a real commercial opportunity starts turning into revenue.

  • Lead & Opportunity: See First Touch and Latest Qualified Acquisition directly on the CRM form, with searchable and groupable acquisition fields.
  • New Customer: Freeze the lead's attribution onto a newly created customer so the acquisition story remains available after conversion.
  • Quotation & Sales Order: Carry the snapshots into a quotation created from the opportunity and keep them visible on the resulting Sales Order.
  • Protect Existing Data: Existing customers are not overwritten with a new lead's attribution, duplicated business records do not inherit stale attribution, and arbitrary backend creates do not read browser tracking state.
Marketing keeps the source. Sales keeps the context. A salesperson can open the opportunity, customer, quotation, or Sales Order and still understand where that business came from.
